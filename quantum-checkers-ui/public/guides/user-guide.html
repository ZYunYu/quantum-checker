Quantum Checkers
User Guide

Create your account, join your classroom and start solving quantum puzzles.

Game: https://quantum-checker.onrender.com/

1. Before you register

Ask your administrator or teacher which classroom to select, for example Fall 2026. They must add the classroom before it can appear in registration.

Have your student ID ready. It must start with an uppercase D followed by digits only, for example D00123456. Keep any zeros after the D. Do not invent an ID or use someone else's.

2. Create an account

  1. Open the game, click Log in, then Create an account. You can also open https://quantum-checker.onrender.com/signup.
  2. Choose a Username. Remember it: this is what you will use to log in.
  3. Enter your email if you wish. Email is optional.
  4. Enter your Student ID, such as D00123456.
  5. Choose your class, such as Fall 2026, from Classroom (optional). Although the website allows Unassigned, choose the class your teacher gives you.
  6. Choose a password with at least 8 characters. Avoid common passwords, an all-number password or a password too similar to your username.
  7. Click Sign up. After successful registration, the login page opens.

If your classroom is missing, ask your administrator to add it, then refresh the registration page. Each username and student ID can be used by only one account. If your ID is already registered, use your existing account or ask your administrator for help.

3. Log in

  1. Enter your Username and password.
  2. Click Log in.
  3. On the main menu, click Start Game and choose a level.

Your student ID and optional email are separate from your login username. They only work in the Username box if you originally chose one of them as your username.

4. Solve a puzzle

The Current board shows the present state. The Target board shows what you need to reach. Use the enabled gate buttons to change Current until it matches Target.

Circle appearanceValue
Black1
White0
Outline0.5
Dashed targetAny value is accepted at this position.
  1. Read the level's instructions.
  2. For X, Z or H, select Left qubit or Right qubit under Apply a gate to.
  3. Click an enabled gate button. The board and move count update.
  4. CZ always acts on both qubits, regardless of which side is selected.
  5. When the target is reached, you see Puzzle solved!. Click Choose another level to continue.

Disabled buttons indicate gates that are not available in this level. If a level lists Extra targets, those conditions must also be met.

Practice with the first example

If the first sample level is still named Flip the left qubit, select Left qubit and apply X once. This should match the target and solve the puzzle. Your administrator may change or replace the example levels.

5. Restart or return later

Click Reset puzzle to start the current level again. This clears that level's moves and solved state. Click All levels to return to the level list.

Your moves are saved to your account as you play. Returning to a level normally resumes your latest attempt. If your administrator changes the puzzle's starting state, targets or available gates, reopening it starts the updated puzzle.

Use Log out on the main menu when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

6. Get help

ProblemWhat to do

My class is missing.Ask your administrator to add it and refresh the registration page.
My username or ID already exists.Try your existing account. Ask your administrator for help if you cannot log in.
I forgot my password.Ask your administrator to reset it. There is no automatic email reset.

The website loads slowly on my first visit.The free website may need time to wake up. Wait and retry.
